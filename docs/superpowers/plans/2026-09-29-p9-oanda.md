# P9 · Forex, gold and silver via OANDA — Plan and rulings

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-28-p8-agent-trading-sessions-design.md` (E4: venues are adapters; §5 P9). Research: OANDA v20 docs (developer.oanda.com), summarised in the HANDOFF.

## Rulings

- **OANDA v20 REST with plain `fetch`.** The official Node client is unmaintained (last pushed 2022). Hosts: `api-fxpractice.oanda.com` (practice) and `api-fxtrade.oanda.com` (live), chosen by the connection's practice/live flag.
- **Connections:** `ExchangeConnection.provider = "oanda"`, with `accountId` (v20 account id, not secret) and an encrypted token. `marketType` is always `swap`, because OANDA trades CFDs with margin, long and short. `quote` is the account currency.
- **Instruments:** catalog forex and commodities map to OANDA names (`EUR/USD` → `EUR_USD`, `XAU/USD` → `XAU_USD`, `WTI` → `WTICO_USD`). Only instruments quoted in the account currency are allowed, so P&L and risk stay in one currency; crosses such as EUR/JPY on a USD account come later with conversion. Whether an instrument exists is decided by the account's `/instruments`, never hardcoded.
- **Position = one OANDA trade** (`SessionPosition.venueTradeId`).
- **Entry:** a FOK market order with `positionFill: OPEN_ONLY`, so it never nets against another trade. Our clientOrderId goes on both the order and the trade client extensions, and the stop is attached with `stopLossOnFill`, so entry and stop are atomic.
- **Unknown outcome:** a timeout or 5xx is resolved via `GET /trades/@<clientId>`, then via `transactions/sinceid` from a cursor taken before sending. OANDA does not dedupe filled market orders, so nothing is ever re-sent. Not found → UNKNOWN_ORDER → the session halts.
- **Stops:** always at the broker. Rounded away from the market to `displayPrecision`. Moved with `PUT /trades/{id}/orders`. A full close cancels them (OANDA does this itself).
- **Closes:** `PUT /trades/{id}/close` with `ALL` or a unit count (partial). P&L, commission and financing come from OANDA's fill (`tradesClosed` / `tradeReduced`), in the account currency.
- **Detection:** every 15 s `syncStops` reads each open trade. A closed one is booked with its reason taken from the closing transaction: stop, target, margin closeout, or manual.
- **Reconciler:** open trades vs the ledger (ids and units). Our trades missing from the ledger count as a mismatch → HALT.
- **Leverage:** `min(mandate max leverage, 1 / instrument marginRate)`. The broker's rate is the truth; Canadian margin tables are not hardcoded.
- **Market hours:** `tradeable = false` means no market rules. An entry that hits MARKET_HALTED is cancelled cleanly, and a cycle with every symbol closed skips the AI call. Closes during a closed market fail and are retried by the monitor.
