# P11b-1 · Strategy Lab engine (`lib/lab`) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A pure TypeScript simulation library that reproduces the 2026-10-20 research inside AlphaBoard: set simulators (the GoldHedgeManager ladder and the Robi-style DCA ladder), a money-management account layer, seeded robustness runs and honest metrics (drawdown with open positions included).

**Architecture:** `lib/lab/*` is pure (no I/O, no Prisma, no `server-only`), works on typed arrays, and is unit-tested on small synthetic bar series with hand-checked outcomes. Two opt-in golden tests (`LAB_GOLDEN_DIR`) run the real research data and must land on the research numbers. Data loading (P11b-2) and the job/UI (P11b-3) consume this API.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vitest. No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-20-p11-honest-numbers-and-strategy-lab.md` (§ P11b). Research scripts it ports: `sim.py`/`sweep.py` (ladder), `account.py`/`mm.py`/`ideas.py` (account + money management), `robi.py` (DCA) — kept in the session scratchpad; their numbers are quoted in the golden tests below.

## Global Constraints

- Pure functions, deterministic for a given seed. Prices and P&L in price units of the instrument; money only in the account layer.
- Inside one bar, when a stop and a target are both touched, the stop wins (conservative).
- Costs are charged per position as a round-trip price amount (`cost`), never hidden.
- A set still open when the data has a hole (gap > 4 h on a weekday, or > 52 h) is dropped, not marked.
- Metrics that cannot be computed are `null`, never 0.

## Tasks

### Task 1 — bars.ts
Tests (synthetic): `makeBars` keeps order and values; `bucketStarts(…, 15)` on 1-minute bars starting 12:07 returns the first index and each :15 boundary; `medianRange` of three 15-min buckets with ranges 2, 4, 9 is 4; `trendH4` is 0 until 200 completed H4 bars, then follows a strictly rising series as +1 and a falling one as −1, using only completed bars (the value at a bar inside a new H4 block equals the previous block's sign); `holeAfter` marks a 5-hour weekday gap and ignores a Friday-to-Sunday gap of 49 h.

### Task 2 — ladder.ts
Tests with hand-built minute bars (unit = 1, so P2 at −1, P1 stop −1.625, target A +1.25, TP after return +0.625, buf 0.0375, cost 0):
- price goes straight to +1.25 → `A`, pnl = 0.75·1.25 + 0.25·(rest), rest = buf when price comes back to entry+buf.
- price to −1 (P2 fills), back to entry → P1 closes at +buf, P2 then reaches entry+0.625 → `B_BACK_TP`, pnl = buf + 1 + 0.625; `returned = true`.
- price to −1 then −1.625 → `B_LOSS`, pnl = −1.625 − 0.625; worst = 2.25.
- same bar touches both P2 stop and the return → stop wins.
- `p2 = null`: −1.625 → `STOP`, pnl −1.625, worst 1.625.
- a hole before resolution → `null`.
- costs subtract `cost` per position (2× after P2 filled); `mae` is the most negative open P&L seen.

### Task 3 — account.ts + metrics.ts
Tests with a fake `sim` (fixed results by index) and bars whose timestamps span two UTC days:
- fixed risk: result pnl = −worst → balance falls by exactly riskPct of the balance; +worst → rises by riskPct.
- slots = 3 → three sets can be open at once and each risks riskPct / 3.
- `maxConsecLosses = 2` → after two losses that day, entries are skipped until the next UTC day.
- `dailyLossPct = 2` → stop for the day once the balance is 2 % under the day's start.
- `cooldownMin = 30` → an entry 20 min after a losing exit is skipped, one 40 min after is taken.
- martingale doubles the risk after a loss and resets after a win.
- `maxDdMtmPct ≥ maxDdRealizedPct`, and MTM uses the set's `mae`.
- `perYearPct`, `maxDrawdownPct`, `profitFactor` small hand cases; `profitFactor([])` is null.

### Task 4 — robust.ts
`mulberry32(1)` is deterministic; `halfSubset` keeps order, is about half the size and is identical for the same seed; `spread([−1, 2, 3])` → median 2, min −1, max 3, positiveShare 2/3.

### Task 5 — dca.ts
Synthetic cube with three symbols and a BTC column:
- a symbol that drops 3 % from its 24 h high is bought at the next open; +1.5 % over average → closed, one episode at depth 0.
- deeper fall to −9 % fills D1 with size 1.8, to −19.5 % fills D2 (deep slot only); TP is then +1 % over the new average.
- shallow slots never reach D2.
- no entries while BTC is down more than `btcLock` in 24 h.
- a symbol whose data ends is closed at its last price.
- cap 100: the reserved ladder budgets never exceed the wallet.
- `maxDdMtmPct` reflects an open position's fall while `maxDdRealizedPct` stays 0.
- optional `stopLoss` closes a position at −stopLoss from the average.

### Task 6 — golden tests (opt-in) and gates
`LAB_GOLDEN_DIR` holds `M1_XAUUSD.csv` (broker export, `utc,open,high,low,close,spread_points,point`) and `binance_1h.bin` + `binance_1h.json` (cube exported from the research). Expected, within tolerance:
- gold, ladder with P2, every 15-min open both directions, cost = median spread + 0.10: **−48 $ per 100 sets (±6)**, return-to-entry **37.4 % (±1 pp)**;
- gold, account (no P2, H4 trend, risk 0.5 %, streak 2, daily 2 %, cooldown 30), 30 random halves: **median ≈ +17 % (±5 pp), ≥ 27/30 positive**;
- Robi DCA from 2021-01-01: **depth share ≈ 86/11/2.6 (±2 pp)**, **max DD (MTM) > 80 %** while **realized DD < 15 %**.
Then `tsc`, ESLint, `npm run test:db`, build; commit; Notion card → Done.
