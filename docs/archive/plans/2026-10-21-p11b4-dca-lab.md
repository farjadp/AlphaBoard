# P11b-4 · Lab page for the multi-coin DCA ladder Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Let Farjad test the "Robi" family (long-only DCA ladder over many coins) himself in Admin → Lab, on a survivorship-free basket of Binance USD-M futures, and see the drawdown with open positions next to the realized one.

**Architecture:** A "basket" is a set of per-symbol hourly series (`LabBasket` + `LabBasketSymbol`, Float32 gzip, each symbol only over its own listed range). A FETCH_BASKET job builds it from data.binance.vision monthly kline zips (delisted symbols included); a DCA job loads it into the existing `runDca` engine (golden-checked in P11b-1). The page gets a "Crypto basket" section: build, settings (Robi defaults), result card with both drawdowns, by-year returns and both equity curves.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-20-p11-honest-numbers-and-strategy-lab.md` § P11b; card "P11b-4".

## Global Constraints
- Survivorship: the universe is every USDT symbol that had a kline file in the chosen "listed by" month — symbols delisted later stay in, and their positions are sold at their last price (engine rule).
- No fabricated bars: a missing month stays missing; a symbol with no data in range is dropped and counted.
- Memory: the cube is held as Float32 (≈ 100 MB for 130 symbols × 5.8 years of hours); the job refuses baskets over 200 symbols.
- Owner-scoped like every Lab table; one basket build at a time per user (job cap applies).
